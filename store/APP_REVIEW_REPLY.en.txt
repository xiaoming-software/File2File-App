--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2) Purpose and target audience
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: File2File is a cross-platform client for encrypted peer-to-peer file
transfer and personal remote disk (NAS-style) management, powered by webrpc.

Problem solved: Users often need to send large files or access a personal disk
without a public IP, complex port mapping, or uploading everything to a third-
party cloud. File2File connects devices over webrpc P2P with encryption by
default.

Target audience: Individuals and small teams who need private device-to-device
file transfer and self-hosted / personal disk access (e.g. home NAS / mywebdisk
server), not enterprise MDM or a public social network.

Value: One app for sessions (chat + file send) and personal drive browsing,
with Token-based login, QR pairing, and account deletion.

4) External services / platforms used for core functionality
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- webrpc (https://www.webrpc.cn / https://api.webrpc.cn/webrpc): Token issuance /
  validation, signaling, and encrypted P2P transport (native webrpc SDK).
- Optional self-hosted mywebdisk-server (user-operated): personal remote disk
  backend reached over webrpc; not a third-party consumer cloud we operate.
- No third-party analytics / ads SDKs for core features.
- No Apple In-App Purchase / no third-party payment processor inside the app.
- No separate authentication provider (no Sign in with Apple / Google required);
  identity is the webrpc device Token.

5) Regional differences
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The app functions consistently across all regions. There are no region-locked
features or region-specific content catalogs. Availability depends only on
network reachability to webrpc services and (for Drive) the user’s own server.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6) Regulated industry / protected third-party material

Not applicable. File2File is a general-purpose P2P transfer / personal disk
client. It does not operate in a highly regulated industry and does not include
protected third-party media catalogs that require special licensing. Users only
transfer or host their own files.
